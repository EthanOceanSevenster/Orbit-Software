Logo options

Four flat, geometric marks. No gradients, no glow and no thin floating rings, which is what made the old one feel generic. Each is shown at 64, 40, 28 and 20 pixels, on white and on navy, because a logo that survives 20px survives anywhere. Aperture is the one in use across the site, the favicon and the email signature. Say the number if you want to switch.

1. Eclipse

A solid disc with a bold ring cut through it. It says orbit without drawing a cartoon planet, and the heavy single-weight shapes stay readable when tiny. The notch on the ring gives it a detail people remember.
